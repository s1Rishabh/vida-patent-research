Four Core Capabilities

What VIDA actually does

Four interconnected capabilities — observed from real patients, inspired by real pain. Together they form a preventive health intelligence system for anyone with a smartphone.

01
📋
Clinical Notes Intelligence
Clinicians write patterns they observe — repeated symptoms, medication responses, behavioral triggers. VIDA learns from anonymized notes using NLP to build predictive symptom profiles. What doctors observe becomes an early-warning system.

Observed first-hand: the repetitive patterns in patient notes are a dataset waiting to be trained on.
NLPClinical DataPattern AI
02
📳
Medication Tremor Sensor
Heavy medication causes subtle body vibrations — tremors invisible to the eye but measurable by a MEMS accelerometer embedded in a phone cover. The VIDA cover captures these signatures at 200Hz, alerting patient and caregiver when patterns shift dangerously.

The sensor is the phone cover. No wearable. No patches. Just pick up your phone.
MEMS SensorPhone Cover HW200Hz
03
🔍
Thermal Body Scan & Score
Using the phone camera + optional FLIR clip-on, the VIDA app scans any body part (knee, shoulder, chest, back) and returns a live Inflammation Risk Score. The BOS artifact removal pipeline from the LaVision thesis cleans the thermal signal.

Your thesis becomes your patent. One model, two domains — gas flow and the human body.
FLIR Thermal IRAttention U-NetRisk Score
04
🌡️
Real-time Mood & State Warning
Optical flow on subtle facial micro-movements, combined with thermal readings (facial temperature shifts measurably with emotional state), enables VIDA to detect distress signals before crisis. The system learns each user's personal baseline and alerts trusted contacts.

Inspired by what happens when no one is watching closely enough.

Patent Novelty

Why this is genuinely new

Exhaustive search across Google Patents, USPTO, EPO, PubMed, and all major sports-tech companies confirms: no system combines thermal IR with dense optical flow for real-time health monitoring. The fusion method has no prior art.

The White Space
"Thermal asymmetry and optical flow kinematics have been studied for a decade — in entirely separate labs, by entirely separate researchers. No one has fused them."
Closest Competitor
ThermoHuman (Madrid)
Uses static FLIR cameras in controlled environments. Athletes must stand still for 30 seconds. No optical flow. No mobile. No real-time fusion.
→ VIDA does all of this on a phone, live, while the patient moves.

Technology

Built on proven science

Every component traces back to validated research. No speculation — just proven methods transferred into a new domain.

🔭
Attention U-Net
95% IoU object masking from BOS thesis at LaVision. Transferred to mask clothing/body artifacts in thermal frames.
From: LaVision Thesis · Stage 1
🌊
Farneback Optical Flow
Dense per-pixel displacement vectors via OpenCV. Detects biomechanical anomalies and facial micro-movements frame by frame.
From: OpenCV · Proven in BOS pipeline
🌡️
FLIR Thermal IR
FLIR ONE clip-on or Lepton XDS module. Maps inflammation via bilateral thermal asymmetry — >0.7°C difference signals risk.
From: ThermoHuman clinical research
📳
MEMS Accelerometer
Embedded in phone cover. Captures sub-threshold medication tremor at 200Hz — invisible to eye, clear to the algorithm.
Novel: No existing product does this
🔀
Multimodal Fusion Layer
The core novelty: thermal hotspot vectors + optical flow kinematics + tremor signal → unified Health Risk Score. Zero prior art.
Novel: White space — 9/10 uniqueness

BOS-style DL Denoising
